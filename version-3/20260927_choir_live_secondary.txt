# 永恒之君求引领 ## Lead On, O King Eternal
@author:Ernest W. Shurtleff；Henry T. Smart；译：胡问宪
@source_url:https://youtu.be/V38lK_vn_lE
--- Chorus
Lead us on, Lord we will follow.
Guide our feet and light our pathway, Lord, lead on.
Take these treasures that we hold, and use them as you will,
O Lord of all creation, lead on.
--- Verse 1
Lead on, O King Eternal, The day of march has come;
Henceforth in fields of conquest Your tents shall be our home.
Through days of preparation Your grace has made us strong,
And now, O King Eternal, We lift our battle song.
--- Chorus
Lead us on, Lord we will follow.
Guide our feet and light our pathway, Lord, lead on.
--- Verse 2
Lead on, O King Eternal, Till sin's fierce war shall cease,
And holiness shall whisper The sweet amen of peace,
The sweet amen of peace;
**
For not with swords loud clashing, Nor roll of stirring drums,
With deeds of love and mercy The heavenly kingdom comes.
--- Bridge
(Lead us on, lead us on) Saints in one accord march together
(Lead us on, lead us on) Joyfully press onward
You and I with one heart sing A triumphant song.
--- Tag
Lead us on, Lord Lead us on, Lord
Lead us on, Lord Lead us on
--- Verse 3
Lead on, O King Eternal, We follow without fear;
Wherever your face appears Gladness breaks like morning.
Your cross is lifted o'er us; We journey in its light.
The crown awaits the conquest; Lead on, O God of might!
--- Ending
Lead us on, Lord we will follow.
Lead us on, O King Eternal, Lead us on!
Lead us on!
